# Count Doodle — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 4 May 2026_

Count Doodle is a personal countdown-timer app for Android. This policy explains
what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

## The short version

**Count Doodle does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.**
All event data, photos, and settings live only on your device. There are no
accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

## What the app stores

When you create or edit an event, the app saves locally on your device:

- The event name, date, optional time, emoji, and background colour you chose.
- A reference to the photo you picked from your gallery, or a photo you took
  with your phone's camera, if you added one.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device.

## Permissions

Count Doodle requests two permissions, both used only when you explicitly
trigger them:

- **Photos / Gallery access** — used when you tap "Pick from gallery" while
  editing an event, so you can choose an image as that event's background.
  The app uses Android's modern photo picker, which limits its access to the
  single image you select.
- **Camera** — used when you tap "Take photo" while editing an event.
  The captured photo is saved into the app's private storage and used only as
  that event's background. The app does not record video, run the camera in
  the background, or upload images anywhere.

If you decline either permission, the rest of the app continues to work
normally.

## What the app does _not_ do

- It does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or
  crash-reporting SDKs.
- It does not contact any server operated by us or anyone else.
- It does not request your contacts, location, microphone, or any other
  sensitive information.
- It does not show ads.
- It is not directed at children under 13, but contains nothing that would be
  inappropriate for a child user.

## Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is sent off your device, data retention is entirely under your
control:

- Delete an event from the app to remove it (and any photo it referenced).
- Uninstall the app to remove everything Count Doodle has stored, including
  the database and any photos taken with the in-app camera.
